Edmonton at a Glance

Edmonton is a vibrant city that combines natural scenery with urban attractions, making it a great destination for short trips or weekend travel. The river valley offers extensive trails and viewpoints that feel surprisingly close to downtown. The city also has lively neighbourhoods with local restaurants, cafés, and shops that are easy to explore on foot.

What Visitors Enjoy

  • Walking and biking the river valley trails
  • Summer festivals like the Fringe and Folk Fest
  • Shopping and food on Whyte Avenue
  • Museums like the Royal Alberta Museum

Best Time to Visit

Summer is the most popular time to visit, with long sunny days and festivals almost every weekend. Fall is also a great choice because the river valley turns gold and the crowds are smaller. Winter is cold, but it is perfect for skating and winter events if you dress warmly.

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Morning: walk a river valley trail and enjoy the views
  2. Late morning: visit the Royal Alberta Museum downtown
  3. Afternoon: explore the Muttart Conservatory
  4. Evening: dinner and dessert on Whyte Avenue
